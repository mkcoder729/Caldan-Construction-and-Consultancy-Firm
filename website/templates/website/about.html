Our Story

CALDAN BUILDER was established in 2008. We have since realized a steady growth in our product portfolio and project range. CALDAN BUILDER has invested heavily in machinery and trained manpower to achieve best in industry standards.

CALDAN BUILDER has continued to strive towards becoming a complete multidisciplinary practice offering our Clients the specialist individual attention and solutions required by an ever-changing project environment.

We believe in a hands-on approach on all projects. That is why a Director of the firm will always be in control of the key functions on our projects.

Our Mission

To offer excellent service in each of our professional disciplines, in accordance with statutory practices, codes of conduct and integrity, thereby developing our team and providing a leading platform from which to service the built environment and in particular, our valued Clients.

Our Vision

To grow our multi-disciplinary team in order to offer a broad spectrum of specialist engineering and construction services to become our Clients' preferred Professional Service Provider (PSP) choice through excellence and efficiency in all aspects of the project life cycle.
