1. Introduction

Caldan Construction and Consultancy Firm uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website, analyse usage patterns, and improve our services. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

We are committed to transparency and complying with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable international standards such as the GDPR.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide valuable information to website owners.

Cookies serve various purposes, including:

  • Session Management — Remembering your login status and preferences
  • Functionality — Enabling specific features and personalisation
  • Analytics — Understanding how visitors use our website
  • Performance — Optimising website speed and performance

5. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Improving User Experience — Remembering your preferences and settings
  • Analytics — Understanding how visitors interact with our website
  • Performance — Optimising website speed and reliability
  • Security — Protecting against fraud and unauthorised access
  • Compliance — Maintaining records of consent and legal compliance

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. You can manage your preferences through:

6.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can typically find these settings under "Privacy" or "Preferences" in your browser menu. You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all cookies
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set
  • Delete specific cookies

6.2 Our Cookie Consent Tool

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your preference will be stored using a functional cookie.

If you change your mind, you can update your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer, or by clearing your cookies and reloading the page.

6.3 Third-Party Opt-Out

For Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on to prevent data collection.

7. Types of Data Collected by Cookies

The cookies we use may collect the following types of information:

  • IP Address — Your device's internet protocol address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser Information — Browser type, version, and settings
  • Device Information — Device type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Usage Data — Pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns
  • Referral Source — How you arrived at our website

We never store sensitive personal information (e.g., passwords or payment details) in cookies.

8. Cookie Duration

Cookies used on our website have different lifespans:

  • Session Cookies — Temporary and deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies — Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted

The duration of each cookie is specified in the cookie tables in Section 3.

9. Data Protection and Privacy

All data collected through cookies is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. We take appropriate measures to protect your data and respect your privacy.

For more information about how we handle your personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our business practices. We will notify you of significant changes through our website or by email.

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and your choices.
